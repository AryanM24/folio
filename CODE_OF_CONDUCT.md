# Code of conduct

Folio follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't tolerated anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions or any other space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the email on [@AryanM24's GitHub profile](https://github.com/AryanM24). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it.
